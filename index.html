Chapter 01 — The Origin

HOW IT STARTED

It began like any other Tuesday: extra cheese, a microwave that had seen things, and absolutely no plans of becoming a legend. Then one bite changed everything — mostly the ceiling, which he now uses as a sitting area.

Bitten by a radioactive spider while eating pizza… Now his room is full of webs, pizza boxes, and responsibilities. He can climb walls, but still can’t clean his room.

Scientists call it “statistically impossible.” His mother calls it “come clean your room right now.” Both are correct.
